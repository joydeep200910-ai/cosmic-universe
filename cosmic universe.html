🕳 Black Hole

A black hole's gravity is so strong that even light cannot escape once it crosses the event horizon.

🌌 Space

Space is completely silent because sound needs air to travel, and space is almost a perfect vacuum.

⚡ Quasar

Quasars are among the brightest objects in the universe and are powered by supermassive black holes.

☁ Nebula

Nebulae are giant clouds of gas and dust where many new stars are born.

🌍 Galaxy
