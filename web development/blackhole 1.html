SUPERMASSIVE BLACKHOLE



Supermassive black holes (SMBHs) are the largest type of black hole, Supermassive black holes (SMBHs) are the largest type of black hole, containing masses from hundreds of thousands to billions of times that of our Sun. They reside at the centers of almost all large galaxies, including the Milky Way, which hosts a dormant SMBH named Sagittarius A* with a mass of about 4 million Suns
