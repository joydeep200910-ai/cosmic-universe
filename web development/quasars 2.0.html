⚡ What is a Quasar?

Quasars are among the brightest and most energetic objects ever discovered, powered by supermassive black holes at the centers of distant galaxies.

The Cosmic Powerhouse

A quasar forms when enormous amounts of gas and dust spiral into a supermassive black hole. The material heats to millions of degrees, producing energy so powerful that one quasar can outshine an entire galaxy.

🕳 Black Hole

Every quasar is powered by a supermassive black hole.

✨ Extreme Brightness

Some quasars shine thousands of times brighter than the Milky Way.

🌌 Great Distance

Most quasars are billions of light-years away from Earth.

⚡ Energy Jets

Powerful jets shoot from the poles at nearly the speed of light.

🚀 Historic Discoveries

Major milestones that transformed our understanding of quasars.

1963 • First Quasar Identified

Astronomer Maarten Schmidt identified 3C 273 as the first quasar, proving these mysterious radio sources were incredibly distant objects.

1979 • Gravitational Lens

The Twin Quasar became the first gravitationally lensed quasar ever discovered.

1990 • Hubble Telescope

Hubble began capturing detailed observations that helped scientists understand quasars.

2022 • James Webb

The James Webb Space Telescope revealed some of the earliest known quasars.
