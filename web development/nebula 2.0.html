Cosmic Clouds of Creation

A nebula is a massive cloud of gas and dust floating through space. These spectacular cosmic structures are the birthplace of stars, planets, and even entire solar systems.

☁️ Gas & Dust

Mostly made of hydrogen, helium, and tiny cosmic dust particles.

🌟 Star Factory

Gravity pulls gas together to create brand-new stars.

🌈 Colorful Beauty

Different gases glow in brilliant shades of pink, blue, and purple.

💥 Stellar Explosion

Some nebulae are formed after giant stars explode as supernovas.
