🌌 What is a Galaxy?

A galaxy is an enormous system of billions of stars, planets, gas, dust, and dark matter held together by gravity. Every galaxy has its own unique shape, size, and history.

⭐ Billions of Stars

Most galaxies contain billions of stars.

🪐 Planetary Systems

Many stars are surrounded by planets.

🌀 Gravity Bound

Gravity keeps everything together.

💫 Supermassive Black Holes

Most galaxies contain one at their centre.

🌌 Types of Galaxies

🌌 Spiral Galaxy

Beautiful spiral arms filled with stars, gas and dust. Our Milky Way is a spiral galaxy.

⭐ Elliptical Galaxy

Smooth and rounded galaxies containing older stars with very little gas and dust.

💫 Irregular Galaxy

Galaxies with no fixed shape, often formed by collisions.

🌍 The Milky Way

Our Home in the Universe

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy that contains our Solar System. It stretches across the night sky and is home to hundreds of billions of stars.
