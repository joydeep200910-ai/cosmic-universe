What is a Black Hole?

🌌 TYPES OF BLACK HOLE

🌠 Intermediate

Larger than stellar black holes but smaller than supermassive ones.

🌌 Supermassive

Found at the centre of most galaxies and can contain millions of solar masses.

✨ Primordial

A theoretical type of black hole thought to have formed shortly after the Big Bang.

🌀 Event Horizon

The event horizon is the invisible boundary around a black hole. Once anything crosses this boundary, it cannot escape—not even light.

It marks the point of no return, where gravity becomes so powerful that escape is impossible.

Amazing Facts

🕳 Light Cannot Escape

The gravity of a black hole is so strong that even light cannot escape once it crosses the event horizon.

⏳ Time Slows Down

According to Einstein's theory of relativity, time moves slower near a black hole.

🌌 Sagittarius A*

A supermassive black hole called Sagittarius A* lies at the centre of our Milky Way galaxy.

☀️ Giant Monsters

💫 Accretion Disk
