🌍 What is Space?

Beyond our atmosphere lies an endless universe filled with mysteries, galaxies, stars, planets and breathtaking cosmic phenomena.

The Infinite Cosmos

Space begins just above Earth's atmosphere and stretches endlessly across billions of light-years. It contains everything from tiny asteroids to enormous galaxies and mysterious black holes.

🌌 Vacuum

Space has almost no air, making it a near-perfect vacuum.

🛰️ Microgravity

Astronauts float because objects are constantly falling around Earth.

⚡ Light Speed

Light travels nearly 300,000 km every second.

♾️ Endless

Scientists still don't know where the universe truly ends.
