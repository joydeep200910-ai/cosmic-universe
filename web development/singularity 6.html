Gravitational Singularity

Where space,time and physics reaach their limits

A gravitational singularity is a theoretical point in spacetime where matter is crushed to an infinite density and zero volume causing gravity and spacetime curvature to become infinite.

🕳️ What is a Singularity?

A singularity is a region where spacetime becomes so extremely curved that our present theories can no longer accurately predict physical behavior.

🌌 Black Hole Centre

In classical General Relativity, a singularity is predicted to exist at the center of a black hole, hidden behind the event horizon.

⚛️ Quantum Gravity

Scientists believe a future theory of quantum gravity may explain what truly happens instead of an actual infinite-density point.

🌌 Amazing Facts

🕳️ Event Horizon

The event horizon is the boundary around a black hole. Once anything crosses it, not even light can escape.

⚛️ Mystery

Scientists still don't know what truly exists at the center of a black hole because General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics don't fully agree there.

🌍 Cosmic Importance

Understanding singularities may help scientists discover a unified theory of physics.
